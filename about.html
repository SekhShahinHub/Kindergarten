Our Journey

How Little Learners Grew Into a Beloved Sanctuary

From a single cozy classroom in 2011 to a vibrant early education hub trusted by over a thousand families.

Founded in 2011

"Every big accomplishment begins with a little spark of curiosity."

Little Learners was founded by early childhood specialist Sarah Jenkins with a clear vision: to create a kindergarten where children feel as loved, safe, and confident as they do at home, while exploring a world rich in creativity and discovery.

Over the years, we have blended play-based learning with structured inquiry, building bright state-of-the-art facilities, lush outdoor adventure gardens, and a passionate team of accredited teachers.

Individualized care tailored to every child's pace
Holistic focus on emotional, social, and cognitive growth
Active parent-teacher collaboration & transparent communication

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our school admissions, care, and daily routines.

We welcome children from 1.5 years to 6 years old across our structured programs: Toddler (1.5-3 yrs), Preschool (3-4 yrs), Pre-K (4-5 yrs), and Kindergarten (5-6 yrs).

Our campus features 24/7 HD CCTV monitoring, biometric access gates, background-checked staff, biometric child check-in/out, and dedicated certified first-aid personnel on site at all times.

We maintain low ratios to ensure personalized warmth and attention: 1:4 for toddlers, 1:6 for preschool, and 1:8 for Pre-K and Kindergarten groups.

Through our secure Parent Mobile App, parents receive real-time updates, activity photos, meal summaries, nap logs, and direct chat access with lead teachers.
