Our Step-by-Step Enrollment Ladder

From first gentle toddler steps to confident kindergarten graduates, follow our step-by-step early childhood milestone pathway.

Daily Rhythm

A Day in the Life at Little Learners

Our balanced daily structure provides predictability, joyful play, nutrition, and peaceful rest.

8:00 AM

Warm Welcome & Morning Circle

Greeting teachers, morning songs, weather chart review, and sharing feelings.

9:30 AM

Discovery & STEM Learning Stations

Small group rotation: phonics corners, building blocks, sensory tables, and guided reading.

11:30 AM

Organic Lunch & Storybook Time

Fresh, chef-prepared organic lunch followed by calm read-aloud story circles.

1:00 PM

Nap & Cozy Quiet Rest

Peaceful nap mats, soft lullaby music, and quiet sensory books for older children.

2:30 PM

Outdoor Playground Adventure

Active play, sandbox building, tricycle rides, and organic garden exploration.

4:00 PM

Afternoon Snack & Parent Pickup

Healthy afternoon snack, reflection on the day, and warm family reunions.
