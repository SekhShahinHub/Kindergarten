Documentation

Admission Requirements Checklist

Please keep the following standard documents ready for a seamless enrollment experience.

Child's Birth Certificate

Official birth certificate copy for age verification and school record.

Passport-size Photographs

4 recent passport-size color photographs of your child.

Parent / Guardian ID

Copy of government-issued photo ID (Passport / Driver's License) of parents.

Medical Records

Updated immunization record and emergency medical contact information.

Previous School Record

Transfer certificate or progress card (if transferring from another kindergarten).

Signed Application Form

Completed enrollment agreement with emergency contact preferences.
